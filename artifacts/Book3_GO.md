### CHAPTER 1: SENT INTO THE WORLD: THE AMBASSADOR'S COMMISSION

> *"Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost: Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen."*  
> — **Matthew 28:19-20 (KJV)**

---

#### 1. The Lifeboat and the Sinking Ship

Imagine standing on the deck of an ocean liner at midnight in the middle of the North Atlantic Ocean. Suddenly, the ship strikes an iceberg, tears open below the waterline, and begins sinking fast into the black, freezing water.

You are thrown into the ocean. Your lungs burn from the freezing cold, and you gasp for air as the dark waves threaten to pull you under. 

Suddenly, a wooden rescue lifeboat cuts through the fog. Strong hands reach down into the icy water, grab your arms, and pull you up over the wooden gunwale onto the dry floor of the boat. They wrap you in a warm wool blanket, hand you a cup of hot broth, and whisper: *"You're safe now!"*

Now, imagine what would happen if the twenty people inside that warm, safe lifeboat sat down in a circle, huddled under their blankets, sang songs about how grateful they were to be saved, and completely ignored the hundreds of drowning men, women, and children screaming for help in the freezing water just three feet outside their boat!

What would you call those people in the lifeboat? 

You would call them callous, selfish, and heartless!

Yet, tragically, this is the exact operational reality of millions of modern Western churches! 

We sit inside our comfortable, climate-controlled church buildings, wrapped in our spiritual blankets of fellowship, singing praises to God—while millions of lost souls in our neighborhoods, workplaces, cities, and jails are drowning in sin, despair, addiction, and spiritual darkness without Christ!

In your royal identity as a prophet, priest, and king, **you were not saved to sit wrapped in a blanket inside a cozy lifeboat! You were saved, equipped, and SENT into the water to rescue drowning souls!**

#### 2. The Supreme Sovereign's Mandate: A KJV Exegesis of Matthew 28:18-20 & 2 Cor 5:20

In Matthew 28:18, King Jesus steps forward to deliver His final marching orders before ascending to Heaven:
> *"All power is given unto me in heaven and in earth. Go ye therefore, and teach all nations..."*  
> — **Matthew 28:18-19 (KJV)**

Look at the foundational keys in this passage:

1. **The Sovereign Authority (*Exousia*)**: Jesus begins by declaring: *"All power (*exousia* — absolute sovereign authority) is given unto me in heaven and in earth."* He does not issue the Great Commission as a weak request or a tentative suggestion. The Supreme Monarch of the Universe commands His army with absolute authority!
2. **The Commission to Disciple (*Matheteuo*)**: In the King James Bible, Jesus commands: *"Go ye therefore, and teach all nations."* The Greek word for *teach* here is **matheteuo**, which literally means **make disciples of, disciple, and reproduce spiritual life!** Jesus did not send us to collect decision cards or count headcounts in pews; He sent us to turn lost sinners into fully devoted, reproducing disciples of Christ!
3. **The Unshakeable Promise**: *"and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world."* Whenever you step out of your comfort zone to share the Gospel, King Jesus promises His personal, miraculous presence walking right beside you!

Furthermore, the Apostle Paul reveals your official Kingdom title in 2 Corinthians 5:20:
> *"Now then we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God did beseech you by us: we pray you in Christ's stead, be ye reconciled to God."*  
> — **2 Corinthians 5:20 (KJV)**

The Greek word for *ambassador* is **presbeuo**. In ancient times, a *presbeuo* was an official diplomatic representative sent by a King to a foreign country. An ambassador does not speak his own opinions, push his own agenda, or seek his own glory. An ambassador speaks with the full authority, message, and backstop of the Sovereign King who sent him!

You are an official **Kingdom Ambassador for King Jesus!**

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      THE KINGDOM AMBASSADOR CODE                       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. SOVEREIGN AUTHORITY : Backed by the King of Kings (Matt 28:18).     │
│ 2. RECONCILIATION VOICE: Proclaiming peace to a fallen world (2 Cor 5).│
│ 3. HOLY GHOST POWER    : Empowered by supernatural witnesses (Acts 1:8).│
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

As a volunteer jail chaplain in Seminole County Correctional Facility and Orange County Jail in Florida, and Vice President of The Gideons International in North Seminole County, I experience this ambassadorial authority every single week. When we step into a high-security jail pod surrounded by steel bars and razor wire, or when we hand a free KJV Testament to a hotel guest or college student, we don't go in our own name or human eloquence. We go as **Ambassadors of King Jesus**, carrying the unshakeable Word of God that breaks chains and transforms lives forever!

#### 4. "Let's Get Real": Overcoming the 3 Myths of Personal Evangelism

Satan loves to paralyze growing believers with three deceptive myths:

1. **Myth 1: "Evangelism is only for gifted, extroverted evangelists."**  
   *The Truth*: While God gives some the spiritual gift of an Evangelist (Ephesians 4:11), **every single born-again Christian is commanded to be a witness!** (Acts 1:8). Sharing your faith is simply *one beggar telling another beggar where to find bread*.
2. **Myth 2: "I need to know all the answers to complex theology before I can share."**  
   *The Truth*: In John 9, the man born blind who was healed by Jesus didn't have a seminary degree. When theologians interrogated him, he simply declared: *"One thing I know, that, whereas I was blind, now I see"* (John 9:25). You only need to know Jesus and your personal story!
3. **Myth 3: "I will offend people if I talk about Jesus."**  
   *The Truth*: When you share the Gospel with genuine love, humility, and respect (1 Peter 3:15), people feel honored that you care about their soul. Truth shared in love brings eternal life!

### CHAPTER 2: YOUR STORY MATTERS: THE POWER OF EYEWITNESS TESTIMONY

> *"And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death."*  
> — **Revelation 12:11 (KJV)**

#### 1. The Courtroom Eyewitness on the Stand

Imagine sitting in a crowded, high-stakes courtroom during a major murder trial. 

On one side sits a high-priced defense attorney wearing a $3,000 suit, spouting legal jargon and arguing technicalities. On the other side sits a veteran prosecutor citing case law and precedent. The jury is confused, bored, and overwhelmed by the legal rhetoric.

Suddenly, the judge taps his gavel and calls a surprise witness to the stand. 

A quiet, ordinary man in simple clothes steps up, places his hand on the Bible, and takes his seat in the witness chair. The prosecutor looks at him and asks: *"Where were you on the night of November 14th at 9:00 PM, and what did you see?"*

The witness looks the jury straight in the eye and replies: *"I was standing right under the streetlamp on the corner of 5th and Main. I saw the defendant walk out of the bank with a black duffel bag, get into a blue sedan, and drive away."*

Notice what just happened in that courtroom!

The ordinary witness did not have a law degree from Harvard. He didn't cite legal precedents, he didn't argue philosophy, and he didn't try to impress anyone with big words. **He simply gave EYEWITNESS TESTIMONY of what he saw and heard with his own eyes!** And his simple eyewitness testimony carried more weight with the jury than ten hours of arguments from the attorneys!

In the Courtroom of the World, Satan wants to convince you that you need a doctorate in theology or seminary training before you can share your faith. 

Silence the Accuser! In your royal identity as a prophet, priest, and king, **you are not called to be the Prosecutor or the Judge—you are called to be an Eyewitness for King Jesus!**

> *"For we cannot but speak the things which we have seen and heard."*  
> — **Acts 4:20 (KJV)**

Nobody can argue with your personal story. An unbeliever may argue with your theology, debate creation vs. evolution, or scoff at religion; but **no person on earth can refute your personal eyewitness testimony of how Jesus Christ transformed your life!**

#### 2. Overcoming the Dragon: A KJV Exegesis of Rev 12:11 & John 9:25

In Revelation 12:11, the Holy Spirit unveils the ultimate spiritual warfare formula for victory over Satan:
> *"And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony..."*  
> — **Revelation 12:11 (KJV)**

Look at the two weapons that defeat the dragon:
1. **The Blood of the Lamb (*Dia To Haima*)**: The objective, finished legal work of Jesus Christ on the Cross that forgives your sins and wipes out your guilt!
2. **The Word of Their Testimony (*Dia Ton Logon Tes Martyrias Auton*)**: The subjective, vocal proclamation of what Jesus did in *your* life!

Notice that victory requires **both**! The Blood of the Lamb purchased your salvation on Calvary, but the *Word of your Testimony* unleashes that salvation into your community! When you open your mouth and share what Jesus did for you, Satan's deception is shattered.

Consider the man born blind in John 9. King Jesus spat on the ground, made clay, anointed the blind man’s eyes, and commanded him to wash in the pool of Siloam. The man washed and came back seeing!

When the religious Pharisees interrogated the healed man and tried to debate theology, the former blind man gave the greatest 1-sentence testimony in human history:
> *"He answered and said, Whether he be a sinner or no, I know not: one thing I know, that, whereas I was blind, now I see."*  
> — **John 9:25 (KJV)**

That is the power of eyewitness testimony! You don't need to know all the answers to complex theological debates. All you need to declare is: *"One thing I know: I was blind, lost, and broken—but Jesus opened my eyes and saved my soul!"*

#### 3. The 2 Master Types of Christian Testimonies

As Vice President of The Gideons International in North Seminole County, Florida, and as a volunteer jail chaplain in Seminole County and Orange County Jails, I hear thousands of personal testimonies. Every Christian testimony falls into one of **2 Master Types**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   THE 2 MASTER TYPES OF TESTIMONIES                    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TYPE A: THE DRAMATIC RESCUE TESTIMONY                                  │
│   • Saved out of deep crime, addiction, prison, or rebellion.          │
│   • Demonstrates God's power to DELIVER from the pit!                  │
│                                                                        │
│ TYPE B: THE PRESERVATION TESTIMONY                                     │
│   • Saved at a young age, raised in church, preserved from ruin.       │
│   • Demonstrates God's power to PROTECT & PRESERVE across a lifetime!  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

##### 1. Type A: The Dramatic Rescue Testimony
- *Example*: In our prison ministry behind jail bars, an ex-offender stands up and shares how he was addicted to drugs, spent ten years in prison, but surrendered to Jesus in a dark cell and was completely set free.
- *Spiritual Impact*: Shows lost, broken sinners that **no one is too far gone for the grace of Jesus Christ!**

##### 2. Type B: The Preservation Testimony
- *Example*: I gave my life to Jesus Christ at age 12 in Indonesia as a 3rd-generation Chinese believer. For 47 years, God preserved my steps, guided my marriage, anchored my family, and allowed me to serve Him in jail chaplaincy and Gideons Bible distribution.
- *Spiritual Impact*: Shows young people and families that **God has the power to keep your life from being destroyed by sin in the first place!**

*Both testimonies carry equal power in Heaven!* Never apologize for your story!

#### 4. "Let's Get Real": Overcoming the 3 Internal Lies About Your Story

Satan loves to silence your witness by whispering three lies:

1. **Lie 1: "My story is too boring. I don't have a dramatic crime or addiction story."**  
   *The Truth*: Being saved at a young age and kept from a lifetime of prison, overdose, and brokenness is a massive, glorious miracle! A preserved life is just as miraculous as a rescued life.
2. **Lie 2: "My past is too dirty. I am ashamed of my old sins."**  
   *The Truth*: Isaiah 1:18 promises: *"though your sins be as scarlet, they shall be as white as snow."* When God forgives your past, your old sins are buried in the depths of the sea (Micah 7:19). Your past scars are now trophies of His resurrection grace!
3. **Lie 3: "No one cares about what happened to me."**  
   *The Truth*: People in your *Oikos* (relational network of family, friends, and co-workers) care deeply about you! When they see the genuine peace, joy, and transformation in your life, your story becomes a magnet pointing them to Christ.

### CHAPTER 3: WRITING YOUR 3-PART TESTIMONY: CRAFTING YOUR 3-MINUTE STORY

> *"But sanctify the Lord God in your hearts: and be ready always to give an answer to every man that asketh you a reason of the hope that is in you with meekness and fear."*  
> — **1 Peter 3:15 (KJV)**

---

#### 1. The Apostle Paul's Courtroom Masterpiece (Acts 26)

Imagine standing in a royal Roman courtroom before King Agrippa, Governor Festus, military commanders, and the elite rulers of Caesarea. 

You are wearing heavy iron chains on your wrists. Your enemies have accused you of treason and heresy, demanding your execution. The King nods his head toward you and says: *"Thou art permitted to speak for thyself."*

If you had five minutes to speak for your life before a King, what would you say?

When the Apostle Paul stood before King Agrippa in Acts 26, he did not launch into a complex, boring legal debate. Paul stretched forth his chained hand and delivered **the greatest 3-part personal testimony ever recorded in human history!**

Examine how Paul structured his speech in Acts 26:
- **Part 1: Life Before Christ (Acts 26:4-11)** — Paul described his background as a strict Pharisee, his religious pride, and his bitter hostility against Christians.
- **Part 2: How He Met Christ (Acts 26:12-18)** — Paul recounted his dramatic encounter on the road to Damascus, the blinding light of heaven, and hearing the voice of King Jesus saying: *"Saul, Saul, why persecutest thou me?"*
- **Part 3: Life Since Christ (Acts 26:19-23)** — Paul shared his transformed life, his new mission, his peace, and his unshakeable passion to preach repentance to all nations!

When Paul finished his 3-part testimony, King Agrippa was so shaken that he leaned forward and cried out: *"Almost thou persuadest me to be a Christian!"* (Acts 26:28).

In your royal identity as a prophet, priest, and king, 1 Peter 3:15 commands you to: **"be ready always to give an answer to every man..."** 

The Greek word for *ready* is **hetoimos**, which means **standing at attention, fully prepared, and equipped!** You should never have to stumble, stutter, or wonder what to say when someone asks you about your faith. In Chapter 3, you are going to write, refine, and master your personal **3-Minute Testimony**!

#### 2. The 3-Part Testimony Blueprint

To craft a clear, compelling testimony that captures attention and exalts Jesus Christ, structure your story into **3 Equal Parts (1 Minute Each)**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   THE 3-PART TESTIMONY FRAMEWORK                       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ PART 1: BEFORE CHRIST (60 Seconds)                                    │
│   • My life, internal emptiness, fears, or self-reliance before Jesus. │
│   • Rule: Keep it honest, but never glorify old sins!                  │
│                                                                        │
│ PART 2: HOW I MET CHRIST (60 Seconds)                                  │
│   • How I heard the Gospel, recognized my need, and surrendered.       │
│   • Rule: Clearly explain repentance & faith in Jesus Christ!          │
│                                                                        │
│ PART 3: LIFE SINCE CHRIST (60 Seconds)                                 │
│   • The peace, transformation, purpose, and hope I experience now.     │
│   • Rule: Keep Jesus as the Hero and end with a bridge question!       │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

As a volunteer jail chaplain in Seminole County Correctional Facility and Orange County Jail, and Vice President of The Gideons International in North Seminole County, Florida, I train team members and inmates to master this 3-part framework. In a jail pod or during a quick conversation at a coffee shop, you don't have forty minutes; you have about two to three minutes before a person's attention drifts. 

When your testimony is concise, sharp, and focused on Jesus, **it cuts through spiritual cynicism like a sharp sword!**

---

#### 3. The 4 Cardinal Rules for a Powerful Testimony

To ensure your testimony is effective and easy for unbelievers to understand, follow these **4 Cardinal Rules**:

1. **Rule 1: Keep It Concise (3 Minutes Max!)**  
   Long, rambling stories lose the listener. Aim for 3 minutes (roughly 300 to 400 words spoken at a conversational pace).
2. **Rule 2: Eliminate Religious Jargon ("Church-Nese")**  
   Unbelievers do not understand internal church buzzwords! 
   - Instead of saying: *"I was under conviction, walked the aisle, got saved, and washed in the blood,"*
   - Say: *"I realized my sin separated me from God, I repented of my wrongdoings, and I trusted Jesus Christ to forgive me and give me a fresh start."*
3. **Rule 3: Keep Jesus as the Hero**  
   Don't spend 90% of your time describing your past sins or how smart you were to find God. You are the saved beggar; **King Jesus is the Savior and the Hero of your story!**
4. **Rule 4: End with a Bridge Question**  
   Never end your testimony with an awkward silence! Transition naturally back to the listener by asking a gentle **Bridge Question**: *"Has anyone ever shared a story like this with you before?"* or *"Have you ever experienced that kind of peace in your own life?"*

### CHAPTER 4: CONVERSATIONAL EVANGELISM: THE SAMARITAN WELL METHOD

> *"Jesus saith unto her, Give me to drink... Whosoever drinketh of this water shall thirst again: But whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst; but the water that I shall give him shall be in him a well of water springing up into everlasting life."*

#### 1. Jacob's Well: Jesus' Masterclass in Witnessing

Imagine sitting under the scorching midday sun in 1st-century Samaria. 

Jesus is exhausted from traveling on foot. He sits down alone by Jacob's Well outside the village of Sychar. Soon, a Samaritan woman approaches carrying a heavy clay water jar. 

In that culture, two massive, impenetrable barriers stood between Jesus and this woman:
1. **The Racial/Cultural Barrier**: Jews hated Samaritans and refused to speak to them or share drinking vessels with them.
2. **The Gender/Moral Barrier**: Rabbis never spoke to lone women in public, and this specific woman had been divorced five times and was currently living with a man who was not her husband.

If a typical religious Pharisee had been sitting at that well, he would have gathered his robes, turned his back, glared at her in self-righteous judgment, or shouted a condemnation at her!

How did King Jesus handle this encounter?

Jesus didn't shout, He didn't judge, and He didn't avoid her! Instead, King Jesus looked at her with divine compassion, opened His mouth, and asked a simple, natural request: **"Give me to drink."**

In that single sentence, King Jesus launched the greatest masterclass in **Conversational Evangelism** in human history! 

Within ten minutes, Jesus bridged the cultural divide, moved from physical water to spiritual water, gently exposed her moral thirst, revealed Himself as the Messiah, and transformed an outcast woman into an unstoppable evangelist who brought her entire city to Christ! (John 4:28-30).

In your royal identity as a prophet, priest, and king, **you are called to master the Samaritan Well Method!**

#### 2. Salt and Grace: A KJV Exegesis of Colossians 4:5-6 & John 4

In Colossians 4:5-6, the Apostle Paul gives us the divine rules for conversational witnessing:
> *"Walk in wisdom toward them that are without, redeeming the time. Let your speech be alway with grace, seasoned with salt, that ye may know how ye ought to answer every man."*  
> — **Colossians 4:5-6 (KJV)**

Look at Paul's military-grade instructions:
1. **"Walk in wisdom toward them that are without"**: Be intentional, polite, observant, and respectful toward unbelievers (*them that are without*).
2. **"Let your speech be alway with grace"**: Never use harsh, argumentative, or arrogant language. Grace makes your words palatable and welcoming.
3. **"Seasoned with salt"**: In ancient times, salt was used to preserve food, add flavor, and **induce thirst**! 

Your everyday conversations should be so seasoned with Holy Spirit grace and wisdom that **they make unbelievers thirsty for King Jesus!**

As Vice President of The Gideons International in North Seminole County, Florida, and as a volunteer jail chaplain in Seminole County and Orange County Jails, I use the Samaritan Well Method every day. 

When sitting across a stainless steel table from an inmate in a jail pod, if I barge in with aggressive religious arguments, his heart locks up like a vault! 

But when I sit down, ask him about his family, ask how he is coping with the stress of his legal case (the physical & emotional realm), and then ask: *"Where do you turn for inner strength when the cell door locks at night?"* (the spiritual realm), his eyes soften, tears form, and the door opens wide for the Gospel!

### CHAPTER 5: OVERCOMING FEAR & HANDLING OBJECTIONS: STANDING BOLD IN TRUTH & GRACE

#### 1. Breaking the Spirit of Fear

Have you ever felt a sudden, paralyzing wall of fear rise in your chest the moment you felt a Holy Spirit nudge to share your faith with a co-worker, neighbor, or family member?

Your heart starts pounding, your palms get sweaty, your throat feels dry, and a flood of panicked thoughts rushes into your mind:
- *"What if they laugh at me?"*
- *"What if they ask a tough theological question I can't answer?"*
- *"What if they think I am a religious fanatic and reject me?"*

So you swallow your words, look down at your shoes, change the subject, and walk away feeling guilty and defeated.

If you have ever experienced that paralyzing fear, listen to the unshakeable truth of God's Word: **That fear is NOT from God!**

In 2 Timothy 1:7, Scripture declares: *"For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind."*

The Greek word Paul uses for *fear* is **deilia**, which literally means **cowardice, intimidation, and demonic paralysis!** Fear is a spirit sent straight from hell to muzzle the mouth of the Church and keep lost souls trapped in darkness.

> *"The fear of man bringeth a snare: but whoso putteth his trust in the LORD shall be safe."*  
> — **Proverbs 29:25 (KJV)**

In your royal identity as a prophet, priest, and king, **you are not a captive slave to the fear of man!** God has equipped you with **Power (*Dynamis*)**, **Love (*Agape*)**, and a **Sound Mind (*Sophronismos*)** to stand bold, gracious, and unshakeable!

#### 2. The 5 Magic Words That Remove All Pressure

The single biggest fear that keeps Christians from sharing their faith is the fear of being asked a hard question they don't know how to answer.

What happens if an atheist asks you about carbon dating, or a skeptic asks about a complex Old Testament passage, or a co-worker asks a deep philosophical question about suffering?

You don't need a Ph.D. in theology, and you don't need to pretend to be an all-knowing genius!

Whenever someone asks a tough question that you cannot answer on the spot, deploy the **5 Magic Words**:

$$\text{"I don't know; let's find out together!"}$$

Look at the supernatural freedom in those 5 Magic Words:
- **It removes all pride and pretense**: You don't have to pretend to know everything.
- **It turns a debate into a joint discovery**: You aren't arguing *against* them; you are inviting them on a journey *with* you!
- **It opens the door for a follow-up meeting**: You can say: *"That is a great question! Let me do some research in the Bible this week, and let's grab a cup of coffee next Tuesday to talk about what I found."*

Those 5 Magic Words turn a scary objection into an ongoing discipleship door!

Imagine walking through a crowded shopping mall parking lot on a blistering hot 95-degree summer afternoon.

In that single moment, **Servant Evangelism has opened a closed heart!**

In your royal identity as a prophet, priest, and king, **Servant Evangelism is your secret Kingdom weapon!** It is the art of demonstrating the love of Jesus Christ by meeting practical physical needs with zero strings attached, thereby earning the right to share the Gospel.

#### 2. Adorning the Gospel: A KJV Exegesis of Matthew 5:16 & Titus 2:10

In Matthew 5:16, King Jesus gives a royal command to His disciples:
> *"Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven."*  
> — **Matthew 5:16 (KJV)**

Look at the Greek word Jesus uses for *good*: **kalos**.

In Greek, there are two words for *good*:
- *Agathos*: Good in moral character or lawfulness.
- *Kalos*: Good that is **beautiful, attractive, heartwarming, and winsome!**

Jesus didn't command us to do dry, legalistic good works. He commanded us to perform **attractive, heartwarming, beautiful deeds (*kalos ergon*)** that compel skeptics and cynics to glorify our Father in Heaven!

Furthermore, the Apostle Paul uses a magnificent phrase in Titus 2:10:
> *"that they may adorn the doctrine of God our Saviour in all things."*  
> — **Titus 2:10 (KJV)**

The Greek word for *adorn* is **kosmeo** (from which we get our English word *cosmetics*!). Just as fine jewelry adorns a bride's wedding gown, **unconditional acts of servant kindness adorn the doctrine of Christ, making the Gospel radiant, beautiful, and desirable to a watching world!**

As Vice President of The Gideons International in North Seminole County, Florida, and as a volunteer chaplain in Seminole County and Orange County Jails, I see how practical service adorns the Gospel. 

When we step into a jail pod and bring reading glasses, hygiene items, writing pads, and genuine respect to inmates who have been discarded and cursed at by society, their hardness vanishes! 

When an inmate holds a new pair of reading glasses so he can read God's Word, he looks up with tears and asks: *"Why are you doing this for me?"* And we reply: *"Because King Jesus loves you, and so do we!"*
