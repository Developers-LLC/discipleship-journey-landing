## Email 2 — Day 2 (Sent 48 hours after opt-in)

**Subject Line:** The question I was too afraid to ask for 3 years
**Preview Text:** This is the real reason I wrote The Discipleship Journey...

---

Hi [First Name],

I hope the guide was helpful. Today, I want to tell you something I've never shared publicly.

When I first became a Christian, I had a question that haunted me for nearly three years. I sat in church every Sunday, nodded along during small group, and smiled when people talked about their faith — but inside, I was terrified.

**My question was this:** *"What if I'm not really saved? What if I prayed the prayer wrong, or I don't feel different enough, or God didn't actually hear me?"*

I was too afraid to ask it out loud. I thought it would reveal that I was a fraud — that I didn't really belong in the church.

It wasn't until a pastor sat with me one afternoon and walked me through 1 John 5:13 — *"These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life"* — that the fear finally broke.

**God wants you to know.** Not to wonder. Not to hope. To *know.*

That conversation changed my life. And it's the reason I wrote *The Discipleship Journey* — to give every new believer the clarity, the community, and the confidence that I didn't have for those three years.

**A few KJV Bible reading tips to get you started:**

The Gospel of John is the best starting point for new believers. Read one chapter per day. Don't rush. Ask God to show you one truth you can hold onto. When you finish John, move to the Psalms — they will teach you how to pray.

Tomorrow, I have something special to share with you — Book 1 of the series is now available, and for a very limited time, it's available at a special introductory price.

With you on the journey,

**[Author Name]**
