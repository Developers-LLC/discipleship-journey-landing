If God can raise up unshakeable Kingdom leaders inside a correctional facility, He will do the exact same in your life as you step into Book 2!

### CHAPTER 2: THE HABIT OF THE WORD: FEEDS YOUR SOUL DAILY

> *"This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success."*
> — **Joshua 1:8 (KJV)**

---

#### 1. Starving at a 7-Course Banquet

Imagine a man sitting at a lavish banquet table overflowing with fresh roast beef, warm bread, crisp vegetables, and sweet fruit. Yet, despite being surrounded by food, he lays his head on the table and slowly starves to death!

Tragically, millions of modern Christians live in spiritual starvation while sitting in the middle of a 7-course spiritual feast!

We live in an age where the Bible is available on every smartphone, tablet, computer, and bookshelf. Yet, recent studies reveal that less than 15% of churchgoers read the Bible daily. We spend three to four hours a day scrolling through social media, watching secular news, and consuming entertainment, but we complain that we *"don't have 15 minutes"* to feed our immortal souls!

Jesus confronted this deception when He declared: *"Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God"* (Matthew 4:4).

If you feed your physical body three meals a day but feed your spiritual soul only one meal on Sunday morning, **your spirit is malnourished!** In your royal identity as a prophet, priest, and king, the first non-negotiable habit of spiritual maturity is building a **Daily Habit of the Word**.

#### 2. Chewing the Cud: A KJV Word Study on Joshua 1:8

In Joshua 1:8, as Joshua prepared to lead Israel into the Promised Land, God gave him the exact secret to spiritual strength and success: *"thou shalt meditate therein day and night."*

The Hebrew word for *meditate* is **hagah**. It does not mean emptying your mind (like Eastern meditation). **Hagah** means to ponder, mutter, speak aloud, and ruminate continuously—exactly like a cow chewing the cud!

A cow swallows grass, brings it back up to chew on it again, and extracts every drop of nutrient and richness from it. When you meditate on KJV Scripture, you don't just skim through words to check a religious box; you chew on a verse all day long at your desk, in your car, and on your walk until its divine truth saturates your heart and mind!

As Vice President of Gideons International in North Seminole County, Florida, and as a volunteer chaplain in Seminole County and Orange County Jails, I have witnessed the supernatural power of Scripture. Inside a prison cell, when an inmate receives a free KJV Testament and begins to *meditate* on its pages, his language changes, his anger melts, and his criminal mindset is destroyed by the washing of water by the Word (Ephesians 5:26)!

#### 2. The Secret Prayer Closet: A KJV Exegesis of Matthew 6:6

In Matthew 6:6, King Jesus commands His disciples: *"enter into thy closet, and when thou hast shut thy door, pray to thy Father which is in secret."*

The Greek word used for *closet* is **tameion**, which referred to an inner private chamber, a secret storage vault, or a royal treasury in a ancient house.

Notice three profound spiritual laws in this verse:

1. **The Secret Place**: God demands a place free from human eyes, performance, and religious showmanship (Matthew 6:5). In the secret closet, you drop your public mask and get totally real with God.
2. **Shutting the Door**: Shutting the door is an intentional act of warfare! You shut out noise, notifications, cell phone alerts, family distractions, and racing thoughts so your mind is focused solely on the King.
3. **Open Reward**: What is whispered in the secret closet is rewarded openly in your character, spiritual power, and answered prayers!

As a volunteer jail chaplain in Seminole County and Orange County Jail, I have witnessed the holy power of the secret prayer closet behind steel bars. In a loud, chaotic jail pod with 60 inmates yelling and televisions blaring, a inmate will pull a towel over his head in his bunk to create his secret *tameion*. With tears falling onto his concrete floor, he shuts the door to the world and pours out his heart to God. I have seen those hidden tears bring miraculous legal grace, restore broken marriages, and fill men with a deep, supernatural peace that passes all human understanding (Philippians 4:7)!

#### 3. The 4-Part A.C.T.S. Conversational Prayer Model

If you have ever knelt to pray and found your mind wandering to your grocery list or work emails after 60 seconds, you need a spiritual roadmap! The **A.C.T.S. Model** is a time-tested, biblical framework that structures your 15-minute daily prayer time into four vibrant movements:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     THE 15-MINUTE A.C.T.S. PRAYER MODEL                │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [A] ADORATION    (3 Minutes) : Praise God for WHO He is.               │
│   "Lord, You are Almighty, Holy, Faithful, Gracious, and Sovereign..." │
│                                                                        │
│ [C] CONFESSION   (2 Minutes) : Clear the spiritual air of sin.         │
│   "Father, forgive my impatient words, my pride, and my anxious heart..."│
│                                                                        │
│ [T] THANKSGIVING (3 Minutes) : Thank God for WHAT He has done.         │
│   "Thank You for saving my soul, providing for my family, and..."      │
│                                                                        │

│   "Lord, give me wisdom at work, heal my loved one, save my neighbor..."│
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

##### 1. ADORATION (Praising God's Attributes)

Start by turning your eyes away from your problems and onto the majesty of God! Praise Him for His divine nature—His omnipotence, His holiness, His unshakeable faithfulness, and His infinite mercy (Psalm 103:1-5).

##### 2. CONFESSION (Clearing the Air)

Unconfessed sin clogs your spiritual lungs and hinders prayer (Psalm 66:18). Agree with God about where you failed in thought, word, or deed. The moment you confess, 1 John 1:9 promises instant, complete cleansing!

##### 3. THANKSGIVING (Counting Your Blessings)

Gratitude destroys bitterness and anxiety. Name specific blessings out loud—your salvation, physical health, daily bread, church family, and past answered prayers (1 Thessalonians 5:18).

##### 4. SUPPLICATION & INTERCESSION (Asking & Standing in the Gap)

Bring your personal requests to God with bold confidence (Hebrews 4:16). But don't just pray for yourself—be a royal priest standing in the gap for your family, pastors, government leaders, coworkers, and lost souls!

### CHAPTER 4: THE HABIT OF GIVING: MANAGING THE KING'S TREASURY

> *"Give, and it shall be given unto you; good measure, pressed down, and shaken together, and running over, shall men give into your bosom. For with the same measure that ye mete withal it shall be measured to you again."*
> — **Luke 6:38 (KJV)**

---

#### 1. The Sea of Galilee vs. The Dead Sea Principle

In the Holy Land of Israel, two famous bodies of water are fed by the exact same river—the Jordan River:

1. **The Sea of Galilee**: Located in the north, it receives fresh water from the Jordan River and continuously pours water out southward into the river valley. Because it has both an **inflow and an outflow**, the Sea of Galilee is vibrant, fresh, and teeming with fish, flora, and biological life!
2. **The Dead Sea**: Located in the south, it receives the exact same Jordan River water. But the Dead Sea has **no outflow**! It absorbs water continuously, but never releases a drop. Over centuries, minerals and salt accumulated until the salinity reached 34%. Today, the Dead Sea is completely stagnant—no fish can swim in it, no trees can grow on its shores, and its water is bitter and poisonous.

In the realm of spiritual growth, human souls operate under this exact same law!

A believer who only receives—consuming sermons, downloading worship songs, receiving pastoral care, and accumulating blessings—but **never gives back** becomes a **Dead Sea Christian**! Their spirit turns bitter, self-centered, critical, and spiritually stagnant.

In your royal identity as a prophet, priest, and king, building the **Habit of Giving** keeps the fresh rivers of the Holy Spirit flowing through your soul like the Sea of Galilee!

---

#### 2. Pressed Down and Running Over: A KJV Exegesis of Luke 6:38

In Luke 6:38, King Jesus reveals the supernatural law of divine return: *"Give, and it shall be given unto you..."*

To understand this verse, look at how grain was bought and sold in ancient Middle Eastern marketplaces:

- When a buyer bought a measure of wheat, an honest merchant did not loosely pour the grain into the measuring basket.
- First, he filled the basket to the brim.
- Second, he **pressed it down** with his hands to eliminate air pockets.
- Third, he **shook the container together** so the kernels settled into every corner.
- Fourth, he poured more grain on top until it was **running over** the edges into the buyer’s folded robe (*bosom*)!

Jesus promises that when you give with an open, generous heart, God measures back to you in that exact same lavish, overflowing proportion!

> *"There is that scattereth, and yet increaseth; and there is that withholdeth more than is meet, but it tendeth to poverty. The liberal soul shall be made fat: and he that watereth shall be watered also himself."*
> — **Proverbs 11:24-25 (KJV)**

As Vice President of Gideons International in North Seminole County, Florida, and as a volunteer jail chaplain in Seminole County and Orange County Jails, I have seen how faithful giving from ordinary Christians transforms destinies. Your giving funds free KJV Testaments placed in hotels, schools, and jail cells—and I have watched indigent inmates in jail pods share their meager commissary items (socks, soap, snacks) with newly arrived prisoners who had nothing. Their generosity in the midst of poverty is a fragrant offering to King Jesus!

Imagine sitting by a roaring campfire on a crisp autumn night. The logs are burning with intense heat and bright orange flames. Now, take a pair of tongs, reach into the center of the fire, pull out one glowing red-hot ember, and set it all by itself on a cold stone off to the side.

What happens to that isolated ember?

Within 60 seconds, the bright red glow fades. Within two minutes, smoke stops rising. Within five minutes, the ember turns ashy grey, cools down to the touch, and dies!

Now, pick that cold, grey ember back up with the tongs and toss it right back into the middle of the burning fire. What happens? Almost instantly, the heat of the surrounding coals ignites the ember, and it catches fire again!

In the spiritual realm, human souls operate under this exact law.

Millions of modern Christians attempt to live as **isolated embers**. #### 2. The Springs of Life: A KJV Exegesis of Proverbs 4:23

In Proverbs 4:23, King Solomon gives a royal directive: *"Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life."*

The Hebrew word for *issues* is **totza'ot**, which literally means the outflowing streams, boundaries, or water springs of a city! In ancient times, if an enemy poisoned the water spring outside a city, the entire population died.

Your heart is the emotional water spring of your soul! Whatever fills your heart will inevitably dictate your words, your priorities, your money, and your ministry direction (Matthew 12:34).

Look at the Prophet Nehemiah in the Old Testament. When Nehemiah heard that the walls of Jerusalem were broken down and the gates burned with fire, he didn't just shrug his shoulders. ### CHAPTER 13: THE ACCOUNTABILITY FACTOR: LOCKING SHIELDS IN SPIRITUAL WARFARE

> *"Iron sharpeneth iron; so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend."*
> — **Proverbs 27:17 (KJV)**

---

#### 1. The Tactics of the Roaring Lion

In the African savanna, a lion hunting a herd of gazelles does not charge straight into the center of the herd. If he rushes the entire herd, fifty fast gazelles will scatter in every direction, and the lion will tire out and go hungry.

Instead, the lion crouches in the tall grass, watches quietly, and waits.

He looks for **one isolated gazelle**—a young calf that wandered away from its mother, an injured male limping behind, or a distracted gazelle straggling fifty yards away from the herd. The moment that gazelle is isolated, the lion pounces!

The Apostle Peter warns us that Satan uses this exact hunting strategy:

> *"Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour."*
> — **1 Peter 5:8 (KJV)**

Satan rarely attacks a believer who is surrounded by a tight, disciplined circle of praying brothers or sisters. The devil waits until you isolate yourself, keep secrets, and stop meeting with your accountability group. The moment you are isolated, he pounces with temptation, lust, fear, or discouragement!

In your royal identity as a prophet, priest, and king, **Accountability is your spiritual armor wall!**

---

#### 2. The Roman Phalanx Shield Wall: A KJV Exegesis of Ephesians 6:16 & Eccl 4:12

To understand why God created accountability, look at the Roman army that dominated the ancient world.

Each Roman soldier carried a massive rectangular shield called a **scutum** (measuring four feet high and two feet wide, constructed of bonded wood, leather, and metal rims).

If a single Roman soldier stood alone in an open field, an enemy archer could easily shoot an arrow around his exposed sides or back. But when the Roman legion formed up on the battlefield, the soldiers stood shoulder-to-shoulder, overlapped their curved shields, and locked them together into a continuous wall of iron and oak called the **Phalanx**!

When enemy archers fired thousands of flaming arrows into the sky, the Roman commander shouted, *"LOCK SHIELDS!"* The arrows slammed into the Phalanx wall and bounced off harmlessly into the dirt.

Paul had this exact military image in mind when he wrote Ephesians 6:16: *"Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked."*

```
                       ┌───────────────────────────────┐
                       │   THE ROMAN PHALANX SHIELD    │
                       └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                                       │
                ┌──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┐
                │                                             │
      ┌─────────┴───────────┐                       ┌─────────┴───────────┐
      │   SOLO SOLDIER      │                       │ LOCKED PHALANX WALL │
      │ (Exposed to Arrows) │                       │ (Impenetrable Team) │
      └─────────────────────┘                       └─────────────────────┘
```

When you lock your Shield of Faith with an accountability partner, **Satan's fiery darts cannot penetrate your life!**

> *"And if one prevail against him, two shall withstand him; and a threefold cord is not quickly broken."*
> — **Ecclesiastes 4:12 (KJV)**

As a volunteer jail chaplain in Seminole County and Orange County Jail, I have seen this military principle proven behind steel bars. In a correctional facility, an inmate who tries to stand as a solo Christian is constantly harassed by gangs, pressure, and contraband. But when two or three Christian inmates form a **Phalanx Cell Pod**—praying every morning, checking on each other's eyes and speech, and holding each other accountable—they become an unshakeable fortress of righteousness!

#### 3. Confession for Healing: A KJV Exegesis of James 5:16

Notice the profound distinction King Jesus makes between forgiveness and healing:

> *"Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed."*
> — **James 5:16 (KJV)**

Read that verse with deep spiritual discernment!

- You confess your sins to **God** for **Forgiveness** (1 John 1:9).
- You confess your faults to **One Another** for **Healing** (James 5:16)!

Secret sin thrives in darkness and shame. The moment you bring a secret struggle (pornography, anger, greed, fear, or addiction) into the light with a trusted, godly accountability partner, **Satan's secret power over your soul is instantly broken!**

#### 5. "Let's Get Real": How to Choose a Godly Accountability Partner

Not everyone qualifies to be your accountability partner! Choose someone who meets these **3 Essential Criteria**:

#### 2. Continuous Growth: A KJV Exegesis of 2 Peter 3:18 & 2 Timothy 2:2

In 2 Peter 3:18, the Apostle Peter gives his final written command to the Church: *"But grow in grace, and in the knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ."*

The Greek word for *grow* is **auxano**. It is in the present active imperative tense, which means **continuously expand, blossom, increase, and never stop growing!**

In the Kingdom of God, there is no plateau. You are either growing forward or drifting backward.

Furthermore, 2 Timothy 2:2 reveals the ultimate goal of all Christian discipleship:

> *"And the things that thou hast heard of me among many witnesses, the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also."*
> — **2 Timothy 2:2 (KJV)**

Notice the **4-Generation Discipleship Chain** in this verse:

1. **Generation 1**: Paul (The Apostle)
2. **Generation 2**: Timothy (The Disciple)
3. **Generation 3**: Faithful Men (The Next Leaders)
4. **Generation 4**: Others Also (The Reached World!)

You were not saved and equipped just to enjoy your spiritual gifts in isolation. You were equipped so that you can turn around and disciple someone else!
