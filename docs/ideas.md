## CHOSEN APPROACH: B — Modern Ministry Platform

### Design Movement
Contemporary Christian Publishing meets High-Conversion Ministry Marketing. References: Lifeway, Zondervan, and premium faith-based SaaS landing pages.

### Core Principles
1. **Trust through gravitas** — Deep royal blue (#0d1f3c) communicates authority, depth, and the eternal. Every section grounds the reader.
2. **Golden urgency** — Golden orange (#f59e0b / #d97706) is used exclusively for CTAs, highlights, and key callouts. It draws the eye and signals action.
3. **Scripture as design element** — KJV verses are typeset as pull-quotes with generous whitespace, treated as visual anchors, not filler.
4. **Conversion architecture** — Every section has one job: move the reader toward the opt-in or the Amazon link.

### Color Philosophy
- **Primary:** Royal Blue `#0d1f3c` — the night sky, the deep sea, the eternal. Communicates depth and trustworthiness.
- **Accent:** Golden Orange `#f59e0b` — the sunrise, the flame, divine light. Used sparingly for maximum impact.
- **Surface:** Warm off-white `#fdf8f0` — parchment-like warmth, not clinical white.
- **Text:** Near-black `#1a1a2e` on light; pure white on dark.

### Layout Paradigm
Asymmetric editorial layout with a left-anchored hero text block, full-bleed section backgrounds alternating between deep navy and warm parchment, and a sticky minimal navigation bar. No generic centered hero.

### Signature Elements
1. **Gold rule dividers** — thin horizontal gold lines with a cross or diamond motif separating sections.
2. **Book cover 3D tilt cards** — the three book covers displayed with a subtle CSS perspective tilt and hover lift effect.
3. **Scripture pull-quotes** — large italic serif KJV verses in golden orange, set against navy backgrounds.

### Interaction Philosophy
Deliberate and reverent. Smooth scroll-triggered fade-ins. Hover states that glow gold. CTAs that pulse subtly to invite action without feeling aggressive.

### Animation
- Scroll-triggered fade-up for section content (60ms stagger per item).
- Book covers: subtle 3D tilt on hover using CSS perspective transform.
- CTA buttons: golden shimmer sweep on hover.
- Hero: text fades in from below on load (200ms delay, ease-out).

### Typography System
- **Display:** `Playfair Display` — elegant serif for titles, book names, and scripture quotes.
- **Body:** `Inter` — clean, readable sans-serif for body copy and UI elements.
- **Hierarchy:** 72px hero title → 48px section titles → 24px subheads → 16px body.

### Brand Essence
*The definitive discipleship series for new believers and small group leaders — grounded in Scripture, built for the modern church.*
**Adjectives:** Authoritative. Welcoming. Transformative.

### Brand Voice
Headlines speak with pastoral warmth and evangelical urgency. CTAs are invitations, not commands.
- Example headline: *"Your faith journey doesn't have to be confusing."*
- Example CTA: *"Yes — Send Me the Free Guide"*

### Wordmark & Logo
A stylized cross integrated with an open book, rendered in gold on navy. Clean, bold, memorable.

### Signature Brand Color
Royal Blue `#0d1f3c` — unmistakably this brand's anchor color.

## Style Decisions
- Dark navy hero with left-aligned text block and book cover fan on the right.
- Golden orange used ONLY for CTAs, price callouts, and scripture accent text.
- Parchment (#fdf8f0) sections alternate with navy sections for rhythm.
- Playfair Display for all headings; Inter for body copy.
